# Review marketing video creative

Use this reference when you design or review marketing video content in this repository. Apply these creative checks alongside existing evidence and approval requirements, and record decisions so another agent or reviewer can inspect their basis.

The [content plan in issue #808](https://github.com/ban12-project/F-Trade-Platform/issues/808) and the [optimization scope in issue #1114](https://github.com/ban12-project/F-Trade-Platform/issues/1114) define the audience, scope, and acceptance criteria. Platform adaptations and missing creative inputs remain open questions for each task.

## Source and scope

This baseline combines Google’s YouTube creative framework with Google Ads Help guidance for video campaigns and short-form vertical placements.

The baseline relies on three checked references:

- **Think with Google article**: [Understanding the ABCDs of effective creative on YouTube](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/future-of-marketing/youtube-video-ad-creative/), published in April 2022 and checked by this project on November 2, 2026
- **Companion playbook**: [ABCDs playbook](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/15987/ABCDs_PDFPlaybook_April2022_Final.pdf), all 25 pages checked on November 2, 2026, using printed page numbers below
- **Google Ads Help reference**: [About the ABCDs of effective video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en) (`answer/14783551`), checked on November 25, 2026, along with its linked [About video ad specs](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13547298) page for specifications and safe zones

These sources describe creative execution principles rather than a guarantee of commercial results. Research figures cited in `answer/14783551` (Google/Kantar, April 2021, reporting up to 30% lift in short-term sales likelihood and 17% lift in long-term brand contribution) describe external study observations, not forecasts for this repository.

Read this guide for changes to these content surfaces:

- Requirements and creative briefs
- Scripts, storyboards, and scene selection
- Captions, brand presentation, and calls to action (CTAs)
- Artificial intelligence (AI) prompts and content review criteria

For infrastructure changes, explain whether they affect these surfaces. If they do not, record why the creative review does not apply.

## Google’s ABCD principles

Use these four principles from the baseline and `answer/14783551` to assess creative execution across devices and viewing surfaces.

| Principle | Core guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Attention | Open fast on the story with tight framing and purposeful pacing, support the message with audio and supers while avoiding competing elements, and keep visuals bright and high-contrast across devices |
| Branding | Introduce the brand or product early and maintain presence throughout, reinforce onscreen branding with audio mentions when sound applies, and draw on authorized branding assets that match the objective |
| Connection | Feature people or realistic product use to make the story relatable, focus on a single clear message instead of crowding the cut, and use fitting narrative tension |
| Direction | Add an intentional call to action (CTA) after setting context, and reinforce onscreen CTAs with voice-over when audio is authorized and supported |

Google also recommends adapting execution to both the placement format and the marketing objective. Choose the pacing mode and primary objective before reviewing creative choices.

## Adapt pacing for vertical short-form video

Google Ads Help (`answer/14783551`) reorders the ABCD insights for YouTube Shorts by impact on the marketing objective instead of alphabetical order: **Connection**, **Attention**, **Branding**, then **Direction**.

Apply these rules when adapting vertical short-form edits (such as YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, or TikTok):

- **Connection first**: Show real product handling or a realistic buyer point of view early to establish relevance before pushing an ask
- **Attention with clarity**: Use tight framing for mobile screens, keep visuals bright and high-contrast, and prevent captions, badges, or CTA buttons from competing in the same frame region
- **Organic branding**: Integrate authorized brand imagery naturally into the story instead of relying on intrusive overlays
- **Relevant direction**: Present a clear CTA tied to the buyer need shown in the video

Because non-YouTube feeds such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and X cannot be assumed to play with sound on, every edit must remain understandable while muted through concise captions and clear product framing. In this repository, the `youtube` export preset renders `1920×1080` landscape video, while `facebook`, `instagram`, and `tiktok` presets export `1080×1920` vertical video.

## Choose guidance for the campaign objective

Both the 2022 playbook (pages 10–24) and `answer/14783551` adapt execution across campaign objectives, adding a **Full funnel** objective alongside Awareness, Consideration, and Action.

| Objective | Attention | Branding | Connection | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness, playbook pages 11–13 | Reinforce visuals with voice, music, and effects | Put the brand front and center and repeat varied brand cues | Feature people and product use; keep the message distinct and focused | Give clear instructions |
| Consideration, playbook pages 16–18 | Retain the opening hook | Hero the product, show how it works, and preserve branding including near the close | Demonstrate relevant benefits in relatable situations | Plant urgency only when backed by verified facts; consider audio reinforcement |
| Action, playbook pages 21–23 | Retain the opening hook | Make the product the ad; keep it visible and avoid distracting brand moments | Be exact and tangible through realistic use cases | Present the ask after context is set; connect the CTA to a confirmed offer |
| Full funnel, `answer/14783551` | Hook attention and focus on elements delivering the single message | Start with varied authorized branding cues and finish on the product | Build audience connection supporting the product | Use clear CTAs and strengthen the ask as the video progresses |

Playbook page 5 introduces a hypothetical dog-treat advertisement and pages 9, 14, 19, and 24 compare versions. Treat those as execution examples rather than product evidence.

For this project, select one primary objective and explain the choice in the review record. Use the following applications as planning templates rather than approved campaign claims:

| Objective | Project application | Proposed evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Introduce the supplier using authorized brand and product imagery | Measure brand recognition with a defined audience and method |
| Consideration | Demonstrate a verified product detail relevant to the buyer’s stated need | Measure visits to the relevant product information |
| Action | Set product context first, then invite the buyer to submit requirements for human review | Measure completed inquiry submissions |
| Full funnel | Combine authorized brand framing, at least one verified product fact, and a closing inquiry CTA across two or more clips | Measure inquiry completion alongside verified product detail engagement |

Confirm that the proposed measurement is available before using it as an acceptance criterion. Record a baseline and comparison method, and do not attribute improvements to creative changes without supporting evidence.

Timing suggestions in external guides depend on video length and placement. Do not invent stock shortages, deadlines, discounts, testimonials, or performance demonstrations to satisfy urgency or action prompts, and require authorized evidence plus human confirmation for commercial terms before including them.

## Project applications and limits

Treat the following checks as project policy rather than additional claims from Google. Evidence, rights, and approval requirements remain mandatory.

| Review area | Project check |
| --- | --- |
| Brief | Identify the audience, platform, objective, pacing mode, available assets, and intended next action; label missing inputs as unknown |
| Opening | Show a relevant product detail or buyer problem; never invent performance claims to attract attention |
| Brand | Use authorized brand assets and make the brand identifiable within the narrative |
| Message | Focus on one buyer need, keep onscreen text concise to avoid competing elements, and bind factual claims to verified product evidence |
| Action | Specify an available next step after context is set, such as submitting fitment details or requesting a human-reviewed quote |
| Platform | Explain assumptions about audio, captions, framing, placement, and viewing context; verify current platform specifications when implementation depends on them |
| Evaluation | Propose an observable outcome tied to the objective; label expected improvements as hypotheses until measured |

Do not import YouTube’s audio assumptions into other platforms without evidence. Do not invent universal timing thresholds, numerical ABCD scores, or conversion guarantees from these sources.

Follow the [product and video evidence architecture](../architecture/evidence-bound-product-video.md) and [marketing video release policy](../decisions/0007-marketing-video-release-policy.md). Keep these boundaries intact:

- Cite factory evidence or human confirmation for engineering facts, compatibility, certifications, lifespan, and safety claims
- Check media rights for the intended use, including advertising, voice-over, music, and AI generation where applicable
- Leave formal quotes and delivery commitments to human confirmation
- Preserve human approval gates; a creative review never authorizes publication
- Keep real customer records and private evidence out of GitHub issues and pull requests; reference repository contracts or synthetic fixtures instead

## Required review record

Include this record in the relevant design, implementation, or review deliverable. Agents must exchange structured records rather than relying on conversational agreement.

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Context | Audience, objective, platform, content surface, and intended action |
| Objective rationale | Primary objective, pacing mode, audience familiarity, relevant guide sections, and reasons for adaptations |
| ABCD decisions | One entry per principle: decision, scene or artifact location, supporting evidence, and review status |
| Evidence | Authorized references for factual claims and assets; identify unverified claims |
| Platform adaptations | Differences from the YouTube baseline and the basis for each assumption |
| Open questions | Missing inputs, responsible reviewer, and whether they block the proposed content |
| Validation | Checks performed, observed results, and any proposed measurement experiment |

Use `supported`, `needs_revision`, or `not_applicable` for each principle’s review status, and explain every `not_applicable` entry. These statuses organize review; they do not establish effectiveness or replace human approval.

## Runtime agent boundary

Repository instructions guide development work; they do not change application model calls. Runtime adoption requires explicit changes across structured contracts, deterministic media analysis, rendering templates, prompts, and tests:

- **Creative and edit contracts**: `lib/video/edit-contracts.ts`, `lib/video/contracts.ts`, `lib/video/creative.ts`, and `lib/video/execution.ts`
- **Deterministic visual and audio analysis**: `lib/video/shot-analysis.ts`, `lib/video/shot-candidates.ts`, `lib/video/visual-sampling.ts`, and `lib/video/sandbox-media.ts`
- **AI cut drafting and rendering**: `workflows/marketing-video-processing.ts`, `remotion/AbcdMarketingVideo.tsx`, `remotion/abcd-types.ts`, `lib/video/remotion-props.ts`, `lib/video/ass-subtitles.ts`, and `lib/video/ffmpeg-renderer.ts`
- **Policy gate**: `lib/video/mvp-policy.ts`, which keeps external video generation disabled by default

When implementing runtime adoption, trace each requirement to its enforcement point. Verify that creative suggestions cannot bypass factual evidence, media rights, or human approval checks.
